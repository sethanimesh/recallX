# RecallX Video Design System

## Style Prompt
Create a light, precise app-store product film grounded in RecallX’s actual study UI. The phone is the main focal point; the browser and Android TV appear as clear supported surfaces. Use open spacing, softened device edges, thin structural rules, small active blue states, and gently rounded white cards. Product screenshots and controls should read as a real vocabulary app, not as a generic tech illustration. Transitions glide, with decisive focus changes and enough stillness to read each card.

## Colors
- Canvas: `#F3F4F6`
- Card: `#FFFFFF`
- Primary text: `#111827`
- Secondary text: `#6B7280`
- RecallX action blue: `#3B82F6`
- Contrast-safe button blue: `#2563EB`, a darker shade within the same blue family
- Confirmation green: `#047857`
- Border: `#E5E7EB`
- Dark TV bezel only: `#12141C`

## Typography
- System sans-serif, one family with regular and semibold weights
- Large word card, strong but unornamented headlines, generous line height

## Motion
- Mobile-first reveal, smooth push/pull camera language, clear device arrivals, short soft UI actions
- Keep motion calm and readable; vary direction and timing between scenes

## What NOT to Do
- No neon, full-screen gradients, fake charts, retention statistics, generic SaaS illustrations, or waveform graphics
- No unreadable miniature copy; present only the study card and minimal navigation labels inside device screens
- No ungrounded grading or learning-outcome claims
